# Files

A proper file manager for the Flipper's **SD card** and **internal storage**.

<p align="center"><img src="images/files.png" alt="The Files page" width="900"></p>

## Browsing

- Switch between the SD card and internal storage on the left.
- **List** or **grid** view, sort by name, size or date, and **filter** the folder you're in.
- Click a file to see its details in the drawer on the right. Text files (like `.txt`, `.ir` and `.sub`) can be read and **edited** right there.
- Hidden files (names starting with a dot) are hidden unless you turn on *Settings → Files → Show hidden files*.

## Uploading

- **Drag files or whole folders** from your computer onto the Files page, or use **Upload** and **Upload folder**.
- Files that are already on the Flipper and unchanged are **skipped**, so a stopped upload carries on where it left off.
- Every transfer shows in the **Transfers** list, where you can cancel or retry it.

## Downloading

Select files or folders and click **Download**. Folders come down with everything in them.

## Organising

- **New folder**, **Rename**, **Delete** (always asks first) and **Move to**.
- **Drag to move**: drag files onto a folder, the folder list or the path at the top.
- **Select all** with Ctrl A (⌘ A on a Mac).

## Folder sync

Keep a folder on your computer mirrored onto the Flipper, like your collection of IR remotes.

1. Click **Folder sync** and pick a folder on your computer and a folder on the Flipper.
2. Click **Sync** whenever you want to bring the Flipper up to date. FATHOM shows you exactly what's new and what's changed before copying anything.
3. Files that are only on the Flipper are left alone, unless you tick the box to remove them and confirm a second time.
