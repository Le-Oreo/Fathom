# Dock

The Dock is FATHOM's home page: everything about the connected Flipper at a glance.

<p align="center"><img src="images/dock.png" alt="The Dock" width="900"></p>

- **Live screen**: a live copy of the Flipper's screen. Click it to open the full **[Screen](Live-Screen)** page.
- **Battery**: charge level, and whether it's charging.
- **SD card**: how much space is used.
- **Firmware**: the version on the Flipper, and whether a newer one is out. If there's an update, an **Update** button appears at the top.
- **Back up** and **Restore**: see **[Backups](Backups)**.
- **Restart** and **Find my Flipper**: restart the Flipper, or make it beep and flash so you can find it.
- **Recent activity**: what you've done lately, like files saved, uploads and screenshots.
- **Library**: how many files of each type you have saved. See **[Library](Library)**.
- **Open on Flipper**: start Sub-GHz, RFID, NFC, Infrared, GPIO or iButton on the Flipper in one click.

Restart and restore always ask first.
