# Privacy

FATHOM has **no telemetry, no analytics and no accounts**. Everything stays on your computer.

It only goes online for these things:

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Official firmware and region data | Flipper's update server, `update.flipperzero.one` |
| Community firmware (Momentum, Unleashed…) | That firmware's GitHub releases |
| Checking for a newer FATHOM | This project's GitHub releases |

Your files, backups and settings are never uploaded anywhere. FATHOM keeps a small log file on your computer to help with bugs, and it leaves out your Flipper's name, its identifiers and your file paths.
